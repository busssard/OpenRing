# Roadmap

**Goal:** an open source, local hub for physical and mental health. Every device and every note
stays on your phone, and the app turns them into a few clear answers that anyone can read.

**Principles**
* Local only: no account, no cloud, no tracking. Backups go where you choose.
* One place for all your devices: the Oura Ring first, more devices step by step.
* Evidence first, then the estimate: you can always see the data behind a number.
* For everyone: simple on the first screen, detail one tap away.

## Now (until the end of 2026)
* **Stable for daily use:** a nightly and a stable channel, so tested changes reach everyone
  weekly, not every few hours.
* **Your data is safe:** automatic backups to a folder you choose (#242), leaving out time when
  someone else wore the ring (#254), Delete all that really deletes (#272).
* **Period tracker, phase 1** (#252): one tap logging, calendar with the expected next period and
  ovulation estimate, reminders, import from drip.
* **Withings watch, phase 1** (#251): check the watch, then sync on a schedule without the
  Withings account.
* **Compare with the official app:** wear a second ring with the Oura app on the same hand for a few
  weeks and show how close our numbers are (sleep stages, heart rate, HRV, readiness).

## Next (first half of 2027)
* **Redesign for everyone:** fewer screens, condensed and intuitive charts, the most important
  answers first, detail one tap away. Built from what the comparison and everyday use show.
* **Life factors:** log what shapes how you feel (meditation, intimacy, holidays, alcohol, stress,
  illness, and your own tags) in one tap, and let them count in readiness, with the effect shown
  for you personally.
* **Period tracker, phase 2:** ovulation from the temperature rise, and how close the estimates were.
* **Withings watch, phase 2:** our sleep and readiness analysis on the watch's heart rate.

## Later
* More devices (scales, blood pressure, other rings and watches), each read locally.
* Mental health: mood and journal notes next to sleep and activity.
* Publishing on F-Droid or IzzyOnDroid for easy, trusted updates.

## Not doing
* No cloud sync, no accounts, no ads, no analytics.
* No diagnosis and no birth control: the app shows patterns and estimates, never medical advice.
