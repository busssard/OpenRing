* Period tracker: Learns from every cycle now handles long cycles (45 days and more). It no longer takes each of them for two cycles with a missed period, which made it expect half the length.
* drip style: the ovulation range now follows its own narrow range for the period, and the card no longer says that about 4 in 5 periods start within drip's range. It says what the range is and how often your periods really started in it.
* Touching a day in a later cycle shows its cycle day counted from that cycle's expected start, instead of numbers like cycle day 85.
* Cycles that vary a lot (for example 21 and 45 days in turn) are no longer all left out of the estimate.
