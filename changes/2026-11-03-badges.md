* Badges for days and nights that were good for you: your best night in 30 days, a restful week, readiness back after low days, phone free evenings, more steps than usual, your activity goal on 4 of 7 days and calm screen days. Each says the numbers that earned it.
* Badges compare only with your own last weeks, never with a made up target, and a day without one says nothing. Days with a left out time count for none.
* Today shows Recent badges from the last 7 days (new ones are marked); tap one to open that day. Settings, Profile and goals, opens the Badges page with a calendar and how often each one came.
* Settings has a switch "Show badges". Nothing about badges is stored, so switching it off deletes nothing.
