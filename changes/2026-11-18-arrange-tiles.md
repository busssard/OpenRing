* Every tab: rearrange the tiles in Settings, Layout of the tabs, Rearrange tiles. Each tile gets a frame and a handle; drag the handle to move the tile, tap it for a menu (Move up, Move down, Fold), which also works with TalkBack. Fold tiles to have them start folded, then Save; Reset puts everything back to the standard.
* Every tile, the score dials included, folds with a tap on its title and shows its key number in one line while folded. Each tile stays open or folded as you leave it.
* Reset layout in Settings puts every tab back at once. The layout is part of a backup; only the look changes, never a number.
