* Settings, Advanced: leave out a time when someone else wore your ring. Its nights and values no longer count in your charts, scores and usual values.
* Before you confirm, a preview says how many nights, days and hours of heart rate or temperature go, and which days are worked out again.
* The ring's data stays stored. Remove a left out time from the list and everything counts again, exactly as before.
* A left out time shows as a grey band on Heart rate today (touch it to read it), and as a Left out card on Today, Sleep and Activity.
