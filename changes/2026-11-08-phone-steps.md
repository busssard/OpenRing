* Your phone counts steps too: allow Physical activity on the Activity tab and the app reads the phone's own step counter about once an hour. Nothing leaves the phone.
* Steps from the phone fill the time your ring or watch was not worn, and the Activity tab shows each reading under the ring's steps; touch it to see the numbers.
* Settings has a third block, Phone, and the main device can be the ring, the watch or the phone.
* No ring? Choose "Use without a ring" in setup: steps, screen time and the period tracker work without one, and Settings keeps "Add a ring" for later.
* Settings, Help, Data check and log lists the phone's steps per day, so you can compare them with your phone's own health app.
