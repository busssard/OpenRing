* Import from drip (Settings, Period tracker): pick the file drip exports and see what it holds before anything is saved: the dates, periods, bleeding and spotting days and morning temperatures.
* Periods of 1 or 2 days are listed on their own line with Correct (add the missing days or join it with a close period) and Ignore. Days you already logged in Open Ring are kept, and the preview says how many differ, with a choice to take the file's values instead.
* Nothing is saved until you tap Import, and "Undo the drip import of" a day removes exactly what that import added. Importing the same file twice changes nothing.
* Morning temperatures by mouth from drip show as their own line on the night temperature card, with their own scale, readable by touch.
