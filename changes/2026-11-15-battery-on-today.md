* Today shows the battery of your ring and your watch right under the scores: the last level, how old that reading is, and about how many days are left when it has been falling steadily since the last charge.
* A low battery (20 % or less) shows in orange with "charge soon"; a reading older than 2 days says the level may be lower now.
* Tap a battery row to see its history: the ring opens its block in Settings, the watch its Watch page.
