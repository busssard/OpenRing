* New Trends page: your sleep, readiness, steps, heart rate, temperature and screen time by week, month or year over your whole history. Open it from Your months at the bottom of each tab.
* Each period shows its average with the middle half of its days as a band and every day as a small dot behind it. Touch a dot to read the month, its range and how many days counted.
* This month against the last says how much longer you slept (or more you walked) and how sure that is. Days without the ring or phone are left out, not counted as zero.
* In Further analysis the chip Month is now called 30 days. The demo has a made up year before its five weeks, so Trends has something to show.
