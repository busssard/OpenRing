* Sleep sounds: the phone can listen through the night and keep short clips of talking, snoring, coughs and other loud moments. Set it up on the Sleep tab (it asks you to agree first and then for the microphone), then tap Listen tonight. Each clip plays on the Sleep tab, stays on this phone and is never in a backup.
* Test the microphone: say one sentence from where you sleep, and the app sets how loud a sound must be to be kept. The sentence is played back once and never stored.
* A notification 30 minutes before your usual bedtime offers to start listening (Settings, Phone, where you also choose how long clips keep their sound: 7 days, 30 days or until deleted).
* Make a full backup before installing.
