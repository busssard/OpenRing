* Slept without your ring and watch? Enter the night by hand on the Sleep tab (up to 30 days back), or from Today's question after a sync: drag In bed and Up on one bar, and add the times you woke up if you remember.
* A night by hand shows as one block "Asleep (by hand)" with its wake ups, gets a sleep score from total sleep, timing and wake ups only, and counts in the charts and in Trends. It can be changed or deleted, with Undo.
* A night from your ring or watch always wins: if one arrives later for the same time, it is shown and the night by hand stays stored until you delete it.
* Make a full backup before installing: this version adds a table for the nights you enter by hand.
