* New: Find my ring (on Today under the score rings, and in Settings, Ring). It shows when the ring was taken off, when this phone last heard it and its battery.
* Hot and cold: a ring fills and clicks get faster as you walk closer to the ring. It tells warmer or colder from the Bluetooth signal, not metres.
* Turn on the spot with the phone against your chest, and an arrow points to where the signal is strongest.
