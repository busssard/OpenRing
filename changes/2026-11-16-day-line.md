* Today's "Your day" card now knows your day and changes every day: first one short sentence with the facts it is based on (for example "You slept 5h 10m, 1h 40m under your need"), then a short thought for the day, written for Open Ring.
* The card checks your day in a fixed order: data out of date, signs of strain (temperature up with resting heart rate up or HRV down, a reason to take it easy, never a diagnosis), a short night or big sleep debt, recovered after low days, a badge, good nights or goal days in a row, late phone use, rest after hard training, a period day (only with the period tracker), a lower, strong, weekend or ordinary day.
* Tap the card or "Why this line" to see every check and its numbers, top to bottom. The line stays the same all day, changes the next day, and none comes back within 30 days.
* Works with the ring, with only the watch (its numbers) and with only the phone (steps and screen time).
