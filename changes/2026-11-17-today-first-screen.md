* Today shows your day's sentence and line on the first screen, with a ring, only a watch or only the phone: the scores, then the battery, then Your day. Badges and the rest follow below.
* The battery is one line per device (level, how old, days left). Tap it to read what the numbers mean and to open each battery's history.
* Without a ring, where the scores come from is one short line that opens on tap, and Add a ring is a small button under Your day.
* Badges all have the same size, two per row, on Today and on the Badges page. On the Badges page, touch a badge to read its rule and open its day.
* Every part of Settings opens and closes the same way: a title, one line with the most used setting or state, and an arrow. All are closed when Settings opens. How it works and Why this line fold the same way.
