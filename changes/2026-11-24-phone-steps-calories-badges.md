* With only the phone, Activity and Today show calories from walking, an estimate from your steps and weight, with how it is worked out and how far off it can be.
* With only the phone, Activity lists your steps of the last 7 days to compare with the phone's own health app.
* Without a ring, Goal reached counts days at your step goal. With only the phone the step goal is set in Settings, Phone.
