* Before each update Open Ring now keeps a copy of your history on the phone, made before the new version touches it. Settings, Automatic backups lists these copies, and "Go back to this copy" puts one back.
* Choose a folder in Settings, Automatic backups, and Open Ring also puts a full backup there every day, week or month while the phone charges. It keeps 3, 5 or 10 of them and never touches other files. These copies stay when the app is removed.
* The copies hold your ring key and all your data, so pick a folder that only you can reach.
