* Find my ring: when the ring stores daytime heart rate, a warm ring only counts as worn with a heart rate. Warm for over an hour without one (on the charger, under a duvet, in the sun) no longer reads as worn: if it had a heart rate when it got warm, the card says "Not sure" and shows the last heart rate; if not, it says when it was taken off and that it reads warm again but is probably not on a finger.
* Find my ring: one odd warm reading or one stray heart rate no longer moves the "taken off" time by hours; the card says which readings it left out.
