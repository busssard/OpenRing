* New on Today, right under Your day: "What was today like?" Tap Meditation, Alcohol, Caffeine late, Stress or Hard workout to log it for the day shown; tap again to take it away, and Undo puts it back.
* More shows every factor (also Intimacy, Holiday, Illness and Travel), logs one factor on several days at once ("Holiday from 3 Nov to 10 Nov"), adds your own factors and lets you choose the chips on the card.
* Sleep shows what you logged before each night, Readiness what you logged the day before, and Body has a month view of your life factors: touch a day to read it.
* Life factors stay on this phone. They are in your full backups and kept when you restore one, never in the Data check or the log, and in the daily CSV only when you tick Include life factors. No score changes.
* Make a full backup before installing: this version adds two tables to the app's database.
