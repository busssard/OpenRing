* With a ring and a Withings watch you get one set of numbers: pick the main device in Settings, Watch (Ring or Watch, Ring at first).
* When the main device was not worn (charging, off your hand), the other one fills that time for steps, distance, heart rate over the day and the night, and a short line under the number says so, for example "Includes 1,240 steps from your watch, 14:00 to 16:00".
* The watch's workouts of the day are listed on Activity.
* The Watch card left Today; the Watch page with everything the watch sent is in Settings, Watch, Open the watch page.
* Readiness and the sleep score stay the ring's. Nothing stored changes, switching the main device only changes what is shown.
