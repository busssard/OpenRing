* Going back to an older day no longer hides the newer days in the 7 and 30 day charts: they keep ending today, and the day you picked is highlighted (its bar, or a line and a bigger dot). Only when you go back further than the chart reaches does it move along with you, one day at a time.
* The numbers for the day you picked stay as they were: its usual values and the averages under the charts still count the days up to that day, and say so when the chart also shows newer days.
* Touching a bar in the ring's 7 day charts (Total sleep, Readiness, Active burn) now reads that day's value.
