* Re analysing your whole history (Re analyse all, a new stage detail level, or after an update) no longer makes nights disappear while it runs: you keep seeing your old nights and days until the new ones are all ready.
* If a re analysis stops part way (the app was closed, the phone ran out of memory), every night and day stays as it was, the error shows in Data check, and the next sync does the re analysis again.
* A sync started during a re analysis waits for it and says "Re analysing your history, this takes a few minutes. The sync starts after it." Live heart rate says to try again then.
