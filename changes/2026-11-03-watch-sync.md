* Your Withings Steel HR can now sync with Open Ring, without the Withings account: check it in Settings, Watch, tap Use this watch for syncing, then Sync the watch now.
* The watch syncs on its own once, twice or 4 times a day, or only when you tap. The ring always goes first.
* A new Watch page (from Today) shows the watch's steps per day, workouts, heart rate, Withings' own sleep, and the battery with how much it falls per day.
* Not tested with a real watch yet. If a sync fails, Settings, Help, Data check and log says which step stopped, without any health values.
