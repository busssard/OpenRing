* New period tracker on the Body tab, and a short card near the bottom of Today. It shows by itself when your profile says female; Settings, Profile, "Show the period tracker" shows or hides it (your logged days stay stored).
* Log a day with one tap (Heavy, Medium or Light, and Ended while a period runs), with Undo. On Today the buttons log the day you are looking at.
* A month calendar with the expected next period, the likely ovulation range and the likely fertile days as bands. Touch and slide to read each day, tap a day to change it (also spotting, or nothing).
* The estimates come from your own cycles and say how sure they are. They are not birth control and not a medical diagnosis.
