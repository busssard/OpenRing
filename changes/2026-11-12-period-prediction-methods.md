* Period tracker: choose how the next period is predicted in Settings, Period tracker, Prediction. Besides Open Ring's own way there are drip style, Recent cycles count more, Follows your last cycle and Learns from every cycle (which also notices a period that was probably not logged).
* The default is Best for you: the app tries every method on your own past periods, each with only the cycles before it, and uses the one that came closest. It may differ from what Open Ring showed before.
* New card on Body, How close the estimates were: each method's average error in days, how often the period started inside its range, a dot for each past period, and how much your temperature helped.
* Demo mode now has 3 years of made up cycles, so the comparison can be tried there.
