* On the days the clocks change, the Activity tab counts every real hour once: the last hour of the long autumn day is no longer lost, and the first hour after the short spring day is no longer counted twice. The chart puts each hour at its clock time.
* Long sitting periods now count when an hour or more of the sitting falls between 08:00 and 22:00. Sitting late in the evening no longer counts. Sitting that goes on past 22:00 counts when an hour of it was before 22:00.
* Impossible activity values from the ring (more than any person can do) are left out instead of adding thousands of calories.
* Days already analysed keep their numbers until they are analysed again (Settings, Help, Re analyse all).
