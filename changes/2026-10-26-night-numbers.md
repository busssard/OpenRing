* Sleep efficiency is now your time asleep out of your time in bed, as the explanation always said. Time the ring gave no sleep stage for counts as time in bed, so a night with missing data no longer shows 100%.
* The Sleep page shows such time as its own row, "No data", with a line saying how it affects efficiency and time to fall asleep.
* A rest in which you did not sleep at all no longer gets "Restfulness 100".
* For rings that work out sleep stages themselves: the stages stay at their real clock times when your bedtime starts earlier or ends later than the ring's own sleep. (Ring 4 does not do this, so it is not affected.)
