* Find my ring has a search walk: hold the phone against your chest and walk slowly through the room. A small map shows where you walked, with dots coloured by the ring's signal (blue weak, warm strong); touch a dot to read its time and signal.
* Once you covered the room, a circle marks where the ring probably is (a place to start looking, about a couple of metres). Turning on the spot still gives the arrow.
* Steps are counted by the app itself, so it needs no new permission. Step length comes from your height in the profile.
