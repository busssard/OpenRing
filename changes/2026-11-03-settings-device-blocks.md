* Settings now has one block for the ring and one for the watch. Each shows a short line (for example "Ring 4, last sync 2 hours ago") and opens when you tap it; both are closed every time you open Settings.
* The Ring block holds the ring's details and Sync now, Find my ring, the sync every 3 hours, sleep stage detail, the measurements on the ring, the ring key, and Use a different key or ring and Factory reset the ring.
* The Watch block holds the main device, the watch's sync, how often it syncs, Check a Withings watch and the watch page.
* Saving a backup file has its own card now, next to the automatic backups. Nothing else changed.
