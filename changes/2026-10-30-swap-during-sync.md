* Restoring a backup's history or turning on demo mode now waits for a running sync to finish ("Waiting for the sync with your ring to finish"), so no downloaded ring data can end up in the wrong history or get wrong dates.
* If it still has to wait after 10 minutes, nothing is changed and the app says what held it up. Back on the demo dialog stops the wait.
