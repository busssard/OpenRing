* Life factors: a new card on Body shows how your nights went after each factor you log (HRV, lowest heart rate, temperature and sleep score, each against your usual).
* Each factor shows its difference with a range, and says "clear" only when the whole range is on one side; below 10 days with it and 30 without, it counts the days.
* Open a factor to see every night of the last 12 months as a dot, and touch one to read it.
* With the period tracker, Period days and After ovulation are compared too.
* Nothing is stored and no score changes. These are patterns in your own nights, not medical advice.
