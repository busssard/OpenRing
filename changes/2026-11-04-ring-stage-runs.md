* For rings that work out sleep stages themselves: when the ring sends its stages in two parts with a short break (an hour or less) between them, the night now keeps the stages of both parts at their own times. Before, the second part was dropped, and the app often fell back to its own estimate.
* The break counts as awake where the ring says you were awake, and as time without a stage otherwise.
* All nights are analysed again once after the update. Ring 4 (which does not send its own stages) keeps the same nights.
